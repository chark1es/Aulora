How to agree

Before a contribution is merged, the CLA bot checks the pull request author and commit authors, including co-authors. Each person must read the published agreement linked in the bot's comment and post the exact acceptance statement it provides from their own GitHub account. The statement has this form, with the revision and SHA-256 filled in by the bot:

I have read and agree to the Aulora Contributor License Agreement, revision REVISION, SHA-256 HASH. I have the authority to make its grants for my contributions.

Acceptance applies to later contributions you intentionally submit under the same agreement text. A change to the text requires a new acceptance. The bot records your GitHub account ID and username, acceptance statement and timestamp, pull request and comment URLs, and agreement revision and hash on the repository's cla-signatures branch. It archives the accepted agreement there as well. These records share the repository's visibility and remain after a comment is edited or deleted.

If an employer owns your contribution, the authority confirmed in your statement must come from that rights holder. Email the project contact to document authorization privately; the bot does not verify employer authorization. See the bot maintenance guide for tracking and check configuration.
