## What the commercial license will cover

- Production use by a for-profit entity, of any size.
- Hosting Aulora for your organization, on your own infrastructure or a
  provider you choose.
- Optional project services such as the official mobile push relay.

It will not cover redistribution of Aulora as a stand-alone product, white
labeling, or resale without a separate agreement.

## How enforcement works

There is no DRM. The admin panel shows a license status (unlicensed,
noncommercial or commercial) and reminds unlicensed commercial installs to buy
a license. You are responsible for complying with the terms.

## Talk to us

For commercial licensing, pricing, a quote, or a question about whether your
use is commercial, open an issue or reach the maintainers through the contact
in the repository README. Until a commercial license is granted in writing,
the noncommercial terms apply.
